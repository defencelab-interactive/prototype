What changes

before the assessment, after it.

We look at what you have and tell you which gaps matter and which don’t, then give you a short list in the order things should be fixed. It is a one-off look rather than a programme: the point is to replace an argument about priorities with a sequence.

before

A report from last year with findings nobody closed, and tools running that nobody has time to read. Nobody can say which of it matters most, so the argument about priorities repeats every quarter.

after

One ordered list: what is exposed now, what an audit would ask for, and what to fix first. The argument about priorities is replaced by a sequence somebody owns.

What you get

a report built to act on.

A document that drives fixes, not one that sits in a drive.

Findings ranked by real business impact

Not by scanner severity — by what it would actually cost you if it were used.

Reproduction steps for your engineers

Precise enough that the person fixing it does not have to guess what was done.

A summary clear enough for your board

The same findings in a form somebody can make a decision from, without translation.
