full-cycle security management

Security work usually arrives in pieces.

One company tests your systems and sends a report. Another sells you a tool and a licence. A third appears when something has already gone wrong. Each piece is fine on its own, but the space between them belongs to nobody — and that's where problems sit unnoticed.

Full-cycle means one team stays with you through all of it. The cycle isn't something we invented — it's the structure international security standards use. What we add is that all six parts of it are ours to run, not split between four suppliers.
